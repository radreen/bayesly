Buying a new computer in the last few years has meant wading through all manner of AI branding nonsense . Most of the AI pitches for laptops are little more than marketing hype, but the launch of Nvidia RTX Spark could bring a meaningful improvement in AI capabilities to the device you use every day. Asus has adopted Nvidia's new chip in its latest ProArt lineup, offering a staggering amount of memory to run local AI models. While it doesn't have a traditional GPU, the Asus ProArt P16 , with up to 128GB of memory, can also be your primary gaming and productivity machine, but this unified experience comes at a high price.
