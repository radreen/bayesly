Bayesly — geometric wordmark

Letters built from circles, stems and diagonals (one weight), then merged / cut like sato and Ardor.

A · Geometric base

Clean geometric letters, one stroke weight, tight spacing.

B · ba shares a stroke

The a's bowl overlaps the b's bowl: two letters, one shared line.

C · Arch y

Both y's are arches with a straight tail (Ardor's arch idea).

D · l flows into y

The l drops, turns through the arch and becomes the y's tail.

E · ba + l→y

Shared stroke at the start, one continuous l→y stroke at the end.

F · ba + tall l→y

As E, with the l rising above the b: the ligature is the accent.
